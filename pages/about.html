About NurtureAI

Empowering healthier communities through Artificial Intelligence, innovation, and accessible maternal and child healthcare.

Who We Are

NurtureAI is an AI-powered healthcare platform designed to support mothers, caregivers, and healthcare professionals with reliable health information. Inspired by the UNICEF StartUp Lab AI for Nurturing Care initiative, our goal is to improve maternal, newborn, and under-five healthcare through innovative technology.

Our Mission

To deliver intelligent, accessible, and community-focused healthcare solutions that empower families and healthcare workers with timely information and support.

Our Vision

To build a future where every mother and child has equal access to quality healthcare through the power of Artificial Intelligence.

Our Core Values

Compassion

Putting mothers and children first in every solution.

Innovation

Using AI to solve healthcare challenges creatively.

Collaboration

Working together with healthcare professionals and communities.

Integrity

Providing trusted, ethical, and secure healthcare support.
