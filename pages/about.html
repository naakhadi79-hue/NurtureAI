About NurtureAI

NurtureAI is an AI-powered healthcare platform designed to support pregnant women, caregivers, and frontline health workers with trusted health information.

Our goal is to improve maternal, newborn, and child healthcare by providing quick, reliable, and easy-to-understand guidance. The platform is designed to work even in communities with limited internet access.

Our Mission

To use Artificial Intelligence to promote healthier pregnancies, safer childbirth, and better child healthcare across Ghana.

Our Vision

A future where every mother and child has access to quality healthcare information, regardless of their location.

Our Core Values

❤️ Compassion

We care deeply about the health and well-being of mothers and children.

💡 Innovation

We use modern AI technology to improve healthcare delivery.

🤝 Accessibility

Healthcare information should be available to everyone.
