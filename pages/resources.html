📚 Health Resources

Helpful information for mothers, babies and caregivers.

🤰 Pregnancy Care

Attend antenatal clinics regularly, eat healthy meals, stay hydrated and get enough rest.

👶 Newborn Care

Breastfeed exclusively for the first six months and keep your baby warm and clean.

🥗 Nutrition

Eat fruits, vegetables, proteins and drink enough clean water every day.

💉 Vaccination

Follow the recommended immunization schedule to protect your child from diseases.

🚑 Emergency Signs

Seek immediate medical care if you experience severe bleeding, high fever or difficulty breathing.

😊 Mental Well-being

Take time to rest, talk to loved ones and seek support if you feel overwhelmed.
