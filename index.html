About NurtureAI

Using Artificial Intelligence to improve maternal, newborn and child healthcare across Ghana.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower mothers, caregivers and frontline healthcare workers with reliable AI-powered healthcare support. NurtureAI aims to improve maternal, newborn and child health by making healthcare information accessible, even in communities with limited internet connectivity.

Our Vision

To become Ghana's leading digital healthcare platform that supports healthier pregnancies, safer childbirth, and stronger communities through responsible Artificial Intelligence.
